### P-101 · Pavement Removal

1. Has the removal operation been controlled to prevent damage to adjacent pavement, base material, and utilities that are to remain? — 101-3.1
2. Is asphalt pavement being cut to the full depth around the perimeter of the removal area? — 101-3.1b
3. If asphalt material is being wasted on the airport site, is it broken to a maximum size of 1 inch? — 101-3.1b
4. Is the joint for each layer of pavement replacement offset 1 foot from the joint in the preceding layer (unless otherwise shown on plans)? — 101-3.1b
5. Is all failed material (surface, base, subbase, and subgrade) being removed and repaired as shown on the plans or as directed by the RPR? — 101-3.1c
6. Is damage caused by the Contractor's removal process being repaired at the Contractor's expense? — 101-3.1c
7. Is milling producing a uniform finished surface? — 101-3.5
8. Is the milling machine operating without tearing or gouging the underlaying surface? — 101-3.5
9. Is the milling machine equipped with automatic grade/slope controls and positive dust control? — 101-3.5
10. Are all millings being removed and disposed of off Airport property (or used/stockpiled as specified)? — 101-3.5
11. Is the milling machine cutting vertical edges without chipping or spalling the remaining pavement edges? — 101-3.5a
12. Does the milling machine have a minimum width of 7 feet and electronic grade control devices? — 101-3.5b
13. Is the machine cutting vertical edges and using positive dust control? — 101-3.5b
14. Does the machine have the ability to windrow or remove millings and load them into a truck? — 101-3.5b
15. Is the milled surface being swept daily and immediately after milling until all residual materials are removed? — 101-3.5c
16. Prior to paving, is the milled pavement being wetted down and thoroughly swept/blown to loosen residual material? — 101-3.5c
17. Are waste materials being collected and removed from the pavement surface and adjacent areas by sweeping or vacuuming? — 101-3.5c
18. Are asphalt pavement surfaces softened by petroleum or failed areas being patched? — 101-3.6a
19. Is damaged pavement removed to full depth and replaced with new asphalt pavement similar to existing? — 101-3.6a
20. Are joints and cracks being repaired in accordance with paragraph 101-3.2? — 101-3.6b
21. Is oil or grease being removed by scrubbing with detergent and washing with clean water? — 101-3.6c
22. Are cleaned oil/grease areas being treated with oil spot primer? — 101-3.6c
23. Is the pavement surface clean and free of dust, dirt, grease, vegetation, oil, or objectionable film immediately prior to surface treatment? — 101-3.6d
24. Is the Contractor performing maintenance to keep pavement in satisfactory condition until acceptance? — 101-3.7
25. Is the surface being kept clean and free from foreign material? — 101-3.7
26. Is the pavement being kept properly drained at all times? — 101-3.7
27. Are joints being cleaned and dried of all scale, dirt, dust, old sealant, curing compound, moisture, and foreign matter prior to sealing? — 101-3.8
28. Has the Contractor demonstrated to the RPR that the cleaning method cleans the joint without damaging it? — 101-3.8

### P-151 · Clearing and Grubbing

1. Are the clearing/grubbing limits properly staked on the ground as shown on the plans? — 151-2.1
2. Is clearing and grubbing being performed sufficiently in advance of grading operations? — 151-2.1
3. Are all cleared materials being disposed of outside the airport limits (unless otherwise directed by RPR)? — 151-2.1.1
4. Are any discarded materials being left in windrows or piles adjacent to or within the airport limits? (Verify NONE exist) — 151-2.1.1
5. Has the RPR approved the disposal manner and location? Does the disposal area create an unsightly or objectionable view? — 151-2.1.1
6. If using private property for disposal, has written permission from the property owner been obtained and filed with the RPR? — 151-2.1.1
7. Is blasting being used for any clearing operations? (Verify NONE) — 151-2.1.2
8. Has the staked area been cleared of all objectionable materials as indicated on the plans? — 151-2.2
9. Have any trees that fell outside the clearing limits been cut up, removed, and disposed of satisfactorily? — 151-2.2
10. Are trees being felled toward the center of the area being cleared to protect standing trees? — 151-2.2
11. Are all trees designated to remain protected from injury during clearing operations? — 151-2.2
12. Are trees, stumps, and brush cut flush with the original ground surface? — 151-2.2
13. Is fence wire neatly rolled and properly stored as directed by the RPR? — 151-2.2
14. In cleared and grubbed areas (excluding deep embankment zones outside paved areas), have all stumps, roots, buried logs, brush, grass, and unsatisfactory materials been removed? — 151-2.3
15. In embankment areas outside paved areas, are sound trees, stumps, and brush cut flush with original ground and allowed to remain? — 151-2.3
16. Are tap roots and projections over 1-1/2 inches diameter grubbed out to at least 18 inches below finished subgrade or slope elevation? — 151-2.3
17. Have all buildings and structures shown on plans for removal been demolished/removed with all materials disposed of off-site? — 151-2.3
18. Have foundations, wells, cesspools, and similar structures been broken down to at least 2 feet below existing ground level? — 151-2.3
19. Has objectionable material that cannot be used in backfill been removed and properly disposed of? — 151-2.3
20. Have holes and openings from removed structures been backfilled with acceptable material and properly compacted? — 151-2.3
21. Do holes in embankment areas have flattened sides to facilitate filling and compaction per P-152? — 151-2.3
22. In areas where grubbing holes exceed proposed excavation depth, have the hole sides been flattened for proper backfilling? — 151-2.3

### P-219 · Recycled Concrete Aggregate Base Course

1. Has the subgrade been compacted to the required density prior to base course placement? — 219-3.1
2. Is each lift of base course 8 inches (200 mm) or less in compacted thickness? — 219-3.3
3. Is the material placed to the lines, grades, and cross-sections shown on the plans? — 219-3.3
4. Is material being placed and compacted within 24 hours of delivery to the site? — 219-3.3
5. Is the compacted base course achieving a minimum of 95% of maximum density (ASTM D1557)? — 219-3.4
6. Is the moisture content within +/- 2% of optimum moisture content prior to rolling? — 219-3.4
7. Are areas failing to meet specified density being reworked and re-compacted until density requirements are achieved? — 219-3.4
8. Is the finished surface smooth and free from ruts, depressions, or irregularities? — 219-3.5
9. Are areas failing smoothness, grade, or crown requirements being scarified to at least 3 inches (75 mm), reshaped, and re-compacted? — 219-3.6
10. Are all final smoothness and grade checks being performed in the presence of the RPR? — 219-3.6
11. Does the finished surface vary no more than +/- 1/2 inch (12 mm) when tested with a 12-foot straightedge? — 219-3.6(a)
12. Is grade and crown being measured on a 50-foot grid? — 219-3.6(b)
13. Is the measured grade and crown within +/- 0.05 feet (15 mm) of the specified grade? — 219-3.6(b)
14. Is the base course thickness within +0 and -1/2 inch (12 mm) of specified thickness? — 219-3.8
15. Are depth tests for thickness being taken by the Contractor in the presence of the RPR? — 219-3.8
16. Are areas deficient by more than 1/2-inch (12 mm) being removed to full depth and replaced at Contractor's expense? — 219-3.8
17. Are surveys being conducted before and after base placement on a minimum 25ft x 25ft grid (if survey method is used for thickness)? — 219-3.8

### P-620 · Runway and Taxiway Marking

**620-3.1 Weather Limitations**
1. Is the pavement surface dry prior to marking operations? — 620-3.1
2. Does the ambient temperature meet the paint manufacturer's minimum requirements? — 620-3.1
3. Does the pavement surface temperature meet the paint manufacturer's minimum requirements? — 620-3.1
4. Is the wind speed at or below 10 mph, or are windscreens in use to shroud the material guns? — 620-3.1
5. Are weather conditions forecasted to remain within the manufacturer's recommendations for application and dry time? — 620-3.1

**620-3.2 Equipment**
6. Is the marking machine an atomizing spray-type or airless-type with automatic glass bead dispensers? — 620-3.2
7. Does the marking machine produce an even and uniform film thickness with clear-cut edges, without running or spattering? — 620-3.2
8. Has the marking equipment (paint and beads) been calibrated today? — 620-3.2

**620-3.3 Preparation of Surfaces**
9. Is the surface free from dirt, grease, oil, laitance, and other contaminants immediately prior to paint application? — 620-3.3
10. If chemicals or impact abrasives were used for surface preparation, was RPR approval obtained in advance? — 620-3.3
11. After cleaning, was the surface swept, blown, or rinsed with pressurized water to remove all grit and debris? — 620-3.3
12. For new pavement surfaces: was the area cleaned by broom, blower, water blasting, or other RPR-approved method to remove all contaminants including PCC curing compounds? — 620-3.3a
13. For removal of existing markings: were markings removed by rotary grinding, water blasting, or other RPR-approved method minimizing damage to the pavement surface? — 620-3.3b

15. For remarking over existing markings: were loose existing markings removed with an RPR-approved method and the surface cleaned of all residue? — 620-3.3c
16. Has the Contractor submitted written certification that the surface is dry and free from all foreign material, along with a copy of the paint manufacturer's application and surface preparation requirements, to the RPR prior to initial marking application? — 620-3.3c

**620-3.4 Layout of Markings**
17. Have the proposed markings been laid out in advance of paint application? — 620-3.4
18. Are the locations of markings to receive glass beads shown on the plans and verified in the layout? — 620-3.4

**620-3.5 Application**

20. Has the RPR approved the layout and condition of the surface prior to paint application? — 620-3.5

22. Do marking edges deviate no more than 1/2 inch (12 mm) from a straight line over any 50-foot (15 m) length? — 620-3.5

24. Are glass beads being distributed upon marked areas immediately after paint application? — 620-3.5
25. Are glass beads being applied at the rate shown in Table 1 and adhering to the cured paint? — 620-3.5

28. Is regular monitoring of glass bead embedment and distribution being performed? — 620-3.5

**620-3.6 Application — Preformed Thermoplastic (when applicable)**
