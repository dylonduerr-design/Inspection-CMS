## 01 45 00.01 - Contractor Quality Control Program (FAA Item C-100)

### Field Inspection Requirements
- Work requiring field testing/approval shall be tested only in presence of City Representative after timely notice
- City Representative has right to witness all off-Site tests; Contractor shall furnish adequate notice
- All items of material, equipment, and work in place subject to inspection by City Representative at site
- Inspection by City Representative does NOT relieve Contractor of performing QC inspections

### Testing & Notification Requirements
- Notify City Representative **not less than 2 Working Days** in advance of operations requiring tests/inspections
- Failure to provide adequate notice: Contractor reimburses Airport for inspection/testing costs
- Make arrangements and pay for additional samples/tests required for Contractor's convenience
- Pay for additional tests required because of unsatisfactory Work
- Pay for tests of substituted materials or retests due to non-compliance

### Testing Agency Requirements
- Provide qualified personnel after due notice
- Notify City Representative and Contractor **immediately verbally**, then by **written report** when tests reveal nonconformance
- Written reports shall include: date, contract info, technician signature, sampling date, temperature/weather, test date, location, test type, results, compliance status

### Contractor QC Testing Plan Requirements
- Include minimum tests and frequencies required by each Specification Item
- Use **statistically-based procedure of random sampling** per ASTM D3665
- City Representative shall be provided opportunity to witness QC sampling and testing
- Include: specification item number, test type, test standard, test frequency, responsibility, control requirements

### Documentation Requirements
- Maintain current QC records of all inspections and tests
- Records must cover both conforming and defective features
- Include: type/number of tests, results, nature of defects, causes for rejection, remedial/corrective actions
- **Legible copies furnished to City Representative DAILY**
- Records shall cover all work placed subsequent to previously furnished records
- **Verified and signed by CQCPA**

### Daily Inspection Reports (by each QC technician)
- Specification item number and description
- Compliance with approved submittals
- Proper storage of materials and equipment
- Proper operation of all equipment
- Adherence to Plans and Specifications
- Summary of corrective actions
- Safety inspection
- Photographs and/or video
- **Signed by responsible QC technician and CQCPA**
- **Provided to City Representative on work day following day of record**

### Daily Test Reports
- Specification item number, test designation, location, date
- Control requirements, test results, causes for rejection
- Recommended remedial actions, retests
- **Submitted to City Representative prior to start of next day's work period**

### Corrective Action Requirements
- CQCP shall indicate appropriate action when process is out of control
- Detail actions to bring process into control
- Include rules to gauge when process is out of control
- Link corrective action requirements to control charts when applicable

### Noncompliance Actions
- City Representative provides written notice of noncompliance
- Contractor must take corrective action after receipt
- If no effective corrective action: Airport may order replacement of ineffective QC personnel or stop operations

## 01 57 23 - Temporary Air and Water Pollution, Soil Erosion, and Siltation Control (FAA Item C-102)

### Pre-Construction Requirements
- **No ground disturbing activities until** Airport approves Site-Specific SWPPP and WDID obtained through CGP
- Submit Site-Specific SWPPP prepared by Qualified SWPPP Developer (QSD)
- Submit schedules for temporary/permanent erosion control work before start of construction
- Work shall not start until erosion control schedules and methods accepted by City Representative
- Notify Airport and Water Board **at least 48 hours** prior to start of applicable Work

### Field Implementation - General
- Exercise every reasonable precaution to control erosion and prevent pollution
- Temporary control measures include: silt fences, berms, dikes, dams, sediment basins, fiber mats, gravel, mulches, grasses, slope drains
- Temporary control may include work outside construction limits: borrow pits, equipment storage, waste areas, temporary plant sites
- Design, install, and maintain to **minimize wildlife attractants** that could attract hazardous wildlife

### Inspection & Maintenance Requirements
- **Inspect erosion control devices immediately before and after each rainfall**
- **Inspect at least daily during prolonged rainfall**
- **Deficiencies shall be corrected immediately**
- Failure to maintain devices: Airport may notify Contractor in writing; if not corrected within **24 hours**, Airport may require discontinuation of other work or remedy deficiencies and deduct costs

### Soil & Waste Storage
- Place soil storage areas near construction entrances, **away from waterways**
- All soil stockpiles shall have appropriate coverings and erosion/flooding control measures
- Place waste storage areas near construction entrances, **away from waterways**
- Waste shall be covered with measures preventing water entry
- Separate hazardous from non-hazardous waste; dry from saturated hazardous waste

### Tracking & Dust Control
- Institute practices to reduce tracking of sediment onto roads (wheel wash facilities)
- Sweep public roadways affected by truck traffic **as needed**
- Implement dust control measures to stabilize exposed surfaces
- **Remove sediments from hard surfaces** within and adjacent to site
- **No debris swept into storm drain systems**

### Concrete Wash-Out
- Provide wash-out areas preventing discharge of high pH/sediment-laden rinse water into storm drains
- Contractor responsible for concrete trucks washing out in unauthorized areas
- Excess wastewater and runoff disposed of off-Airport at appropriate treatment facility

### Silt Fence Requirements
- Install at locations shown on approved SWPPP and as directed
- Maintain alignment and condition throughout use
- Remove upon completion or as directed
- Relocate as necessary if approved

### Diversion Dikes
- Minimum **18-inch high and 24-inch wide**
- Exclude storm water runoff from Work areas
- Capture all rainwater falling within contaminated Work areas
- Hydro-seed with Airport-approved seed at **100 pounds/acre**
- Mulch using straw or similar materials

### Temporary Seeding
- All areas cleared of vegetation **longer than 30 calendar days** shall be hydro-seeded
- Application rate: **100 pounds/acre** with Airport-approved seed
- Mulch using straw or similar materials

### Construction Entrance
- Construct equipment decontamination pad **prior to any excavation or earthmoving**
- Clean if clogged with soil
- Remove pad at completion; top soil and seed area

### Slope Drains
- May be constructed of pipe, fiber mats, rubble, concrete, asphalt, or other adequate materials

### Lined Sump Pits
- Construct polyethylene-lined sump pits for collection of surface water entering hazardous work areas
- Construct so liquids may be removed using vacuum tanker truck

### Reporting & Inspections
- Submit all reports required to comply with Airport's Construction SWPPP
- Inspections conducted **weekly and quarterly**, and **before, during, and after rain events**
- Comply with Airport's Construction SWPPP, Inspection Frequency and Reporting, Section 500.4.14

### Construction Sequence
- Incorporate permanent erosion control features at earliest practicable time
- Perform permanent seeding/mulching as soon as substantial areas of exposed slopes available
- Schedule clearing/grubbing so grading and permanent erosion control can follow immediately if possible
- Temporary measures required if permanent measures cannot immediately follow grading
- City Representative may limit area of erodible earth material exposed based on Project conditions

### Prohibitions
- **No discharge** of liquid, solution, wash water, or operational effluent into any storm, industrial, or sanitary drainage systems without prior written approval from BPPP Section
- Do not remove, cut, deface, injure or destroy trees/shrubs without Airport's written permission
- Fuels, lubricants, bitumen, raw sewage, wash water from concrete mixing, and other harmful materials shall **NOT be discharged into waterways, impoundments, or channels**

### Geotextile Fabric (Silt Fence) Properties
- Weight: 3 oz/yd² minimum
- Grab Tensile Strength: 100 lb minimum
- Mullen Burst: 235 psi minimum
- UV Stability: >90% strength retained after 500 hrs
- Permeability: 0.01 cm/sec
- Apparent Opening Size: #50 U.S. sieve

## 02 22 26 - Potholing

### Field Excavation Requirements for Utility Verification
- Exploratory excavations by potholing to verify or discover actual locations and size of existing underground utilities
- Potholing consists of excavations at existing underground utilities within construction area

### Submittal Requirements
- Method and Schedule: Submit proposed method of potholing and schedule to RPR for approval minimum 5 working days prior to commencement
- Survey Records: Submit to RPR within 2 working days after completion of potholing in each area

### Equipment and Methods

**Hand Digging:**
- Excavating pothole by manual means with hand-held, non-mechanical equipment (shovel)

**Vacuum Excavation:**
- Air or water pressure to break up soil; vacuum device to collect spoil
- Contractor determines air or water based on site and environmental characteristics
- Heavy clay may require water vacuum excavation
- Use air vacuum if mud from water vacuum cannot be disposed of properly
- Use air vacuum if damage to utilities (e.g., cutting cables) will occur with water vacuum

### Potholing Execution Requirements
- **DO NOT pothole any fuel line without fuel line owner/operator representative present** (unless directed otherwise by RPR)
- After RPR approval of schedule, commence potholing to determine actual location and elevation of all utility lines within limits of excavations
- Exercise extreme care and caution to not damage any pipes, conduit, ductbank, directly buried cables and wires during excavation

### Pothole Dimensions
- Surface dimensions: no more than 18 inches in diameter OR 18" x 18" square (unless otherwise approved by RPR)

### Backfill and Restoration Requirements
- Immediately backfill and compact upon completion of potholing in each area
- In existing paved areas: backfill and restore to match existing pavement
- Restore potholes within 24 hours after utility located
- Potholes may only be left open if approved by RPR
- Open potholes (where approved): fence with construction fencing 5 feet from edge of hole; delineate with approved barricades equipped with flashing lights

**Aviation Fuel Line Potholes:**
- Backfill to original subgrade elevations with aggregate base
- Compact to 90% of maximum dry density
- Lifts not to exceed 6 inches to top of subgrade
- Compact backfill above subgrade to match pavement section compaction requirements (minimum 95% maximum dry density)

## 02 41 19 - Preparation/Removal of Existing Pavements (FAA Item P-101)

### Field Milling, Removal, and Surface Preparation Requirements

### Removal of Existing Pavement

**Concrete Pavement Removal:**
- Full depth saw cuts perpendicular to slab surface
- Saw through full depth of slab including any dowels at joint
- Remove pavement and install new dowels as shown on plans and per specifications
- Where perimeter of removal limits not on joint and no dowels present: perimeter saw cut full depth of pavement
- Remove pavement inside saw cut by methods not causing distress to pavement to remain
- Concrete slabs damaged by under breaking: remove and replace as directed by RPR
- Protect edge of existing concrete pavement against which new pavement abuts from damage at all times
- Spall and underbreak repair per plans
- Recompact and/or replace any underlaying material to remain per plans

**Asphalt Pavement Removal:**
- Cut to full depth of asphalt pavement around perimeter of area to be removed
- If material wasted on airport site: break to maximum size of 1 inch
- Unless otherwise shown on plans: remove pavement so joint for each layer of pavement replacement offset 1 foot from joint in preceding layer

### Preparation of Joints and Cracks Prior to Overlay/Surface Treatment
- Remove all vegetation and debris from cracks to minimum depth of 1 inch
- Fill all cracks greater than 1/4 inch wide with crack sealant per ASTM D6690
- Underfill crack sealant minimum 1/8 inch, not to exceed 1/4 inch to minimize contamination
- Remove any excess joint or crack sealer on pavement surface

### Removal of Foreign Substances/Contaminants Prior to Overlay, Seal-Coat, or Remarking
- Remove rubber, fuel spills, oil, crack sealer, at least 90% of paint, and other foreign substances affecting bond of new treatment
- Methods: chemicals, high-pressure water, heater scarifier (asphaltic concrete only), cold milling, or rotary grinding
- Removal methods shall not cause major damage to pavement, structures, or utilities
- **Major damage defined as:** changing properties of pavement; removal of asphalt causing aggregate to ravel; removing pavement over 1/8 inch deep

### Cold Milling
- Perform with power-operated milling machine or grinder capable of producing uniform finished surface
- Operate without tearing or gouging underlaying surface
- Equip with automatic grade and slope controls
- Positive means of dust control required
- Remove and dispose all millings off Airport property

**Patching:**
- Milling machine capable of cutting vertical edge without chipping or spalling edges of remaining pavement
- Positive method of controlling depth of cut
- RPR lays out area to be milled with straightedge in increments of 1 foot widths

**Profiling, Grade Correction, or Surface Correction:**
- Milling machine minimum width of 7 feet
- Equipped with electronic grade control devices to cut surface to grade specified
- Tolerances: within +0 inch and -1/4 inch of specified grade

**Clean-Up:**
- Sweep milled surface daily and immediately after milling until all residual materials removed from pavement surface
- Prior to paving: wet down milled pavement and thoroughly sweep and/or blow surface to loosen residual material

### Removal of Pipe and Other Buried Structures
- Remove types of pipe as indicated on plans
- Legally dispose of pipe material off-site in timely manner following removal
- Backfill trenches with material equal to or better in quality than adjacent embankment
- Trenches under paved areas: compact to 95% of ASTM D1557
- Trenches outside paved areas: compact to 90% of ASTM D698

## 03 30 00 - Concrete for Miscellaneous Structures (FAA Item P-610)

### Scope
- Plain and/or reinforced concrete for airfield signage and light can bases, NAVAID foundations, drainage structures, and other miscellaneous airfield concrete (excluding airfield pavements)
- Includes High Early Strength (HES) cement concrete for early opening of runway/taxiway areas

### Field Placement Requirements

**Concrete Mixture Specifications:**
- Minimum compressive strength: 4,000 psi at 28 days (ASTM C39)
- Minimum cement content: 470 lbs/cubic yard
- Maximum water-cementitious ratio: 0.45 by weight
- Air content: 5% ± 1.2% (ASTM C231)
- Maximum slump: 4 inches (ASTM C143)

**High Early Strength (HES) Concrete:**
- Minimum 3,000 psi at 24 hours (general HES applications)
- Minimum 3,000 psi within 2 hours for conduit trenches and base cans in existing asphalt areas

**Placement Time Limits:**
- Concrete shall be placed no later than **1 hour** after water is added to the mix
- Place as soon as practical after mixing
- Rate of delivery must allow uninterrupted placement

**Placement Methods:**
- Place during daylight hours unless otherwise approved
- Do not drop concrete from height exceeding **5 feet (1.5 m)**
- Deposit as nearly as practical in final position to avoid segregation
- Place on clean, damp surfaces free from running water

### Reinforcement Placement and Spacing

**Placement Requirements:**
- Accurately placed as shown on plans, firmly held in position during concrete placement
- Bars fastened together at intersections
- Supported by approved metal chairs
- Minimum clear spacing per ACI 318
- Upper bars placed directly above lower bars where multiple layers indicated

**Minimum Concrete Cover:**
- Footings and concrete formed against earth: **3 inches**
- Concrete exposed to earth/weather (No. 6 bars and larger): **2 inches**
- Concrete exposed to earth/weather (No. 5 bars and smaller): **1-1/2 inches**

### Formwork Requirements
- Forms must be inspected and approved by RPR before concrete placement
- True to line and grade, mortar-tight, sufficiently rigid to prevent displacement and sagging
- Smooth surfaces free from irregularities, dents, sags, and holes
- Internal form ties arranged so no metal shows in concrete surface or discolors when weathered
- Forms wetted with water or non-staining mineral oil immediately before concrete placement
- Forms constructed to be removed without injuring concrete surface

### Concrete Curing Requirements
- Cure per ACI 308R, Guide to External Curing of Concrete
- Protect concrete from damage until project acceptance

### Field Testing and Acceptance Criteria

**Contractor QC Requirements:**
- Monitor concrete consistency; collect each batch ticket
- Check temperature
- Perform slump tests on each truck at project site (ASTM C143)

**QA Sampling and Testing (by RPR):**
- Sample concrete per ASTM C172
- Test slump (ASTM C143), air content (ASTM C231)
- Make/cure compressive strength specimens per ASTM C31
- Test compressive strength per ASTM C39

### Weather Limitations for Placement

**Cold Weather (below 40°F/4°C):**
- Do not mix concrete when air temperature below 40°F without RPR approval
- If approved: heat aggregates/water and place concrete at **50°F-100°F (10°C-38°C)**
- Follow ACI 306R, Cold Weather Concreting

**Hot Weather (above 85°F/30°C):**
- Follow ACI 305R, Hot Weather Concreting

**General:**
- Retempering concrete by adding water or any other material is **not permitted**

### Finishing Requirements
- All exposed concrete surfaces shall be true, smooth, and free from open/rough areas, depressions, or projections
- Horizontal plane surfaces brought flush to proper elevation
- Finished top surface struck-off with straightedge and floated

### Protection Requirements
- Protect concrete from damage until project acceptance
- Contractor held responsible for any defective work resulting from freezing or injury during placing and curing; must replace at their expense

## 31 23 00 - Excavation, Subgrade, and Embankment (FAA Item P-152)

### Field Excavation Procedures
- No excavation shall start until work has been staked out by Contractor and RPR has obtained survey notes of elevations and measurements of ground surface
- Contractor shall verify accuracy of existing ground surface by verifying spot elevations
- Spot elevations within 0.1 foot of stated elevations for ground surfaces, or within 0.04 foot for hard surfaces shall be considered "no change"
- All areas to be excavated shall be stripped of vegetation and topsoil; topsoil shall be stockpiled for future use
- Grade shall be maintained so surface is well drained at all times
- **Blasting is NOT allowed**

### Undercutting Requirements
- Rock, shale, hardpan, loose rock, boulders, or other unsatisfactory material shall be excavated to minimum depth of 12 inches below subgrade or to depth specified by RPR
- Unsuitable materials shall be disposed off the airport

### Subgrade Preparation
- In areas where subbase or base course is to be placed, top 12 inches of subgrade shall be compacted to:
  - **Not less than 100% of maximum density for non-cohesive soils** (PI < 3 per ASTM D4318)
  - **95% of maximum density for cohesive soils** as determined by ASTM D1557
- Stones or rock fragments larger than 4 inches in greatest dimension will not be permitted in top 6 inches of subgrade

### Embankment Construction
- All sod and vegetative matter shall be removed from surface upon which embankment is to be placed
- Cleared surface shall be broken up by plowing or scarifying to minimum depth of 6 inches and compacted
- Sloped surfaces steeper than 1V:4H shall be plowed, stepped, benched, or broken up so fill material will bond with existing material

### Control Strip Requirements
- First half-day of construction of subgrade and/or embankment shall be considered control strip
- Contractor shall demonstrate materials, equipment, and construction processes meet specification requirements
- Maximum compacted thickness may be increased to maximum of 12 inches upon Contractor's demonstration
- Full operations shall not begin until control strip has been accepted by RPR

### Lift Placement & Compaction
- Material shall be constructed in lifts as established in control strip, but **not less than 6 inches nor more than 12 inches of compacted thickness**
- No lift shall be covered by subsequent lifts until tests verify compaction requirements have been met
- Earthwork operations shall be suspended when satisfactory results cannot be obtained due to rain, freezing, or other unsatisfactory weather conditions
- **Frozen material shall not be placed in embankment nor shall embankment be placed upon frozen material**
- Material shall not be placed on surfaces that are muddy, frozen, or contain frost
- Material in each layer shall be within ±2% of optimum moisture content before rolling

### Compaction Requirements
- Rolling operations shall continue until embankment is compacted to:
  - **Not less than 95% of maximum density for non-cohesive soils**
  - **90% of maximum density for cohesive soils** as determined by ASTM D1557
- Under all areas to be paved, embankments shall be compacted to depth of 12 inches and to density of not less than 95% of maximum density per ASTM D1557

### Field Testing Frequencies
- Density tests shall be taken by Contractor for every **3,000 square yards** of compacted embankment for each lift
- Tests for moisture content and compaction taken at minimum of **1,000 S.Y. of subgrade**
- In-place field density determined per ASTM D1556 or ASTM D6938 using Procedure A (direct transmission method)

### Proof Rolling
- After compaction completed, subgrade area shall be proof rolled with:
  - 20 ton Tandem axle Dual Wheel Dump Truck loaded to legal limit with tires inflated to 150 psi, OR
  - 15 ton Proof Roller with tires spaced not more than 32 inches on-center with tires inflated to 125 psi
- Apply minimum of **two coverages** under pavement areas
- Soft areas that deflect more than 1 inch or show permanent deformation greater than 1 inch shall be removed and replaced with suitable material or reworked

### Surface Tolerances
- **Smoothness:** Finished surface shall not vary more than ±½ inch when tested with 12-foot straightedge applied parallel and at right angles to centerline
- **Grade:** Grade and crown measured on 50-foot grid and shall be within ±0.05 feet of specified grade

## 31 23 19 - Dewatering

### Field Dewatering Requirements
- Contractor shall be responsible for continuous control of ground water at all times during construction, including Saturdays, Sundays, holidays, and during periods of labor strikes
- Dewatering operation shall be continuous to keep excavated areas free from water during construction, while concrete is setting and achieves full strength, and until backfill has been placed to sufficient height to anchor work against possible floatation
- Continue dewatering during backfilling operations such that groundwater is at least **one foot below the level of the compaction effort** at all times
- **No compaction of saturated materials will be allowed**
- Contractor shall maintain general ground water level at all times within **2 feet of initial ground water level**
- Contractor shall minimize water from seeping through excavation; surface runoff shall be controlled to prevent entry of water into excavation

### Equipment Requirements
- Contractor shall at all times have on project site sufficient pumping equipment for immediate use, including **standby pumps** for use in case other pumps become inoperable
- Contractor shall provide sufficient number of pumps to hold groundwater level at elevation of not less than one foot below lowest elevation of concrete, pipe, duct or other material to be placed
- Dewatering devices must be adequately filtered to prevent removal of fines from the soil

### Hydrostatic Pressure Relief
- Where deep excavations are made, Contractor shall maintain safe hydrostatic pressure level directly below excavated areas
- Contractor shall be responsible for preventing all hydrostatic pressure build-up under newly-placed slabs and walls until concrete has attained its 28-day compressive strength

### Water Disposal & Testing
- Contractor shall not discharge any ground water to the Bay, nor any sediment, debris, or pollutants into Airport sewer and storm drainage systems
- Ground water may be discharged into storm drainage system after settlement in holding tank and if TPH is below level allowed by Water Quality Control Board
- Contractor shall furnish, install and operate **settling tanks** during all excavation operations in sufficient quantity to handle any foreseeable flow
- When water is encountered, perform water testing for total petroleum hydrocarbon (TPH) and diesel, gasoline, and jet fuel; provide test results by certified laboratory within one day to RPR

## 31 23 23 - Backfill and Geotextiles

### Field Backfill Procedures
- Backfill material shall consist of suitable site excavated material or imported material, free from vegetable matter, rubbish or other substances which will prevent compaction
- Unless otherwise noted, fill shall be placed in **8-inch uniform layers** around pipe before compaction
- Water shall be removed from or added to backfill as necessary to achieve optimum moisture content for **90% density of compaction** as established by laboratory tests per ASTM D1557, Method C
- Upper 3 feet of backfill below subgrade or ground surface shall be compacted to **not less than 95% density**
- Construction shall be suspended when satisfactory results cannot be obtained on account of weather or other unsatisfactory conditions
- Contractor shall blade or slope fill to provide proper surface drainage so no water is collected and no ponding is formed

### Geotextile Placement - Filter Fabric
- Install filter fabric in underdrain trenches, around drain pipes, or other locations to prevent undesirable movement or wash-off of fine soil
- Before placing filter fabric, remove loose or extraneous material and sharp objects
- Place fabric longitudinally along alignment without wrinkles
- Overlap adjacent edges of rolls at least **2 feet**; overlap ends of rolls at least 2 feet in direction material is spread
- Make repairs by placing new piece of material over damaged area with at least **3 feet of overlap** from edges of damaged area
- **Do NOT:** stockpile material on fabric; place more fabric than can be covered in 72 hours; operate equipment or vehicles directly on it

### Geotextile Placement - Soil Stabilization Fabric
- Where Bay Mud or unsuitable materials at bottom of trenches or at subgrade are encountered:
  - **Trenches:** Overexcavate to depth of 12" or deeper as directed; line bottom of trench with soil stabilization fabric extending full width of trench; backfill with drain rock material or larger aggregate to depth of 12"; wrap drain rock with fabric with 12" minimum overlap before placement of fill material
  - **Large Areas:** Overexcavate to depth of 12" or deeper; line area with stabilization fabric; backfill with drain rock material to depth of 12"; line top of drain rock with second layer of stabilization fabric before placement of fill material

### Geotextile Placement - Pavement Stabilization Fabric
- Prior to placing fabric, existing pavement shall be cleaned of all vegetation, sand, dirt, gravel, and water
- Fabric shall **NOT** be placed under overlays of less than 1-1/2 inches of compacted thickness in 1st lift
- Cracks between ¼ inch and ½ inch shall be filled with crack filler; wider cracks repaired with fine hot mix asphalt
- Placement of fabric shall be made only when:
  - Ambient air temperature is above 50°F and rising
  - Pavement is dry and pavement temperature is 40°F and rising
- Fabric shall be placed into asphaltic binder with minimum of wrinkles; large wrinkles (1 inch and larger) shall be slit and lapped in direction of paving
- All fabric shall be broomed to maximize pavement contact and remove air bubbles
- Width of liquid asphalt application shall be fabric width plus 4 inches
- Fabric shall overlap **2 to 6 inches at longitudinal joints** and **no more than 2 inches at transverse joints**
- No joints shall be lapped with more than two layers of fabric

## 31 32 13.13 - Cement Treated Subgrade (FAA Item P-156)

### Field Mixing & Placement
- Prior to any treatment, subgrade shall be constructed as specified in Item P-152 and shaped to conform to typical sections, lines, and grades
- Mixing machine must give visible indication at all times that it is cutting, pulverizing and mixing material uniformly to proper depth over full width of cut
- Cement shall be uniformly spread only over area where initial mixing operations and compaction can be completed during same workday
- **Cement shall not be applied when wind conditions are detrimental to proper application**
- **Motor grader shall NOT be used to spread cement**
- Adequate moisture shall be added to cement/soil mixture to maintain proper moisture content
- Cement shall not be left exposed for more than **one (1) hour** after distribution
- Mixing and pulverization shall continue until soil cement mixture contains **no clods greater than 1-1/2 inches** in size

### Weather Limitations
- **Do not construct subgrade when weather conditions detrimentally affect quality of materials**
- **Do not apply cement unless air temperature is at least 40°F (4°C) and rising**
- **Do not apply cement to soils that are frozen or contain frost**
- **Do not apply cement when conditions are too windy to allow even distribution**
- If air temperature falls below 35°F (2°C), protect completed treated areas against freezing
- Remove and replace any damaged portion of completed treated area with new material

### Control Strip Requirements
- First half-day of construction shall be considered control strip
- Contractor shall demonstrate materials, equipment, and construction processes meet specification requirements
- Full operations shall not continue until control strip accepted by RPR

### Compaction Requirements
- Moisture content shall be within tolerance as specified (optimum +2%, 0 to +1% tolerance)
- Field density of compacted mixture shall be at least **95% of maximum density** per ASTM D558
- **Compaction shall begin within 30 minutes after mixing cement into subgrade**
- **All compaction operations shall be completed within 2 hours from start of mixing**

### Finishing & Curing
- After final lift or course has been compacted, bring to required lines and grades
- **Not later than 24 hours after completion of final finishing, surface shall be cured by:**
  - Application of emulsified asphalt, OR
  - Kept continuously moist for period of **7 days** with fog-type water spray, OR
  - Application of curing compound or other moisture retention methods as approved by RPR
- Sufficient protection from freezing shall be provided for at least **7 days** after construction

### Surface Tolerances
- **Smoothness:** Finished surface shall not vary more than ±½ inch when tested with 12-foot straightedge
- **Grade:** Grade and crown measured on 50-foot grid and shall be within ±0.05 feet of specified grade

### Field Testing Frequencies
- Testing frequency: minimum of **one compaction and thickness test per 1,000 square yards** of stabilized subgrade, but not less than **four tests per day** of production
- Depth of stabilization shall be measured by Contractor with no less than **2 tests per day** of material placed

## 31 32 13.19 - Lime Treated Subgrade (FAA Item P-155)

### Mixing Procedure
- **Preliminary mixing:** Full depth of treated subgrade shall be mixed with approved mixing machine; mixing machine shall make two coverages
- Water shall be added during mixing to provide moisture content approximately **3% to 5% above optimum moisture** to ensure chemical reaction
- After mixing, subgrade shall be lightly rolled to seal surface and help prevent evaporation
- Water content shall be maintained above optimum for **minimum of 4 to 24 hours** (mellowing period)
- **Final mixing:** After required mellowing time, material shall be uniformly mixed by approved methods; pulverize until 100% of soil particles pass one-inch sieve and 60% pass No. 4 sieve

### Weather Limitations
- **Lime shall not be applied unless air temperature is at least 40°F (4°C) and rising**
- **Lime shall not be applied to soils that are frozen or contain frost**
- Protect completed lime-treated areas against detrimental effects of freezing if air temperature falls below 35°F (2°C)

### Compaction Requirements
- Compaction shall immediately follow final mixing operation with mixture compacted **within 1 to 4 hours after final mixing**
- Material shall be at moisture content specified during compaction (water tolerance: +2%, -0%)
- Field density of compacted mixture shall be at least **95% of maximum density** of laboratory specimens per ASTM D698
- Perform in-place density test to determine degree of compaction **between 24 and 72 hours after final compaction** and the 24-hour moist cure period

### Finishing & Curing
- After final lift has been compacted, bring to required lines and grades
- **Finished surface shall not vary more than ½-inch** when tested with 12-foot straightedge
- Completed section shall be **moist-cured for minimum of seven (7) days** before further courses added or any traffic permitted

### Surface Tolerances
- **Smoothness:** Finished surface shall not vary more than ±½ inch when tested with 12-foot straightedge on 50-foot grid
- **Grade:** Grade and crown measured on 50-foot grid and shall be within ±0.05 feet of specified grade

### Field Testing Frequencies
- Testing frequency: minimum of **one compaction and thickness test per 1,000 square yards** of lime treated subgrade, but not less than **four tests per day** of production
- Depth and amount of stabilization measured by Contractor with **no less than 2 tests per day**

## 31 32 16.16 - Soil Stabilization and Dust Control

### Field Application Requirements
- **No work shall be done during wet or unsuitable weather**
- Mixing and placing of soil stabilizer treated surface will **not be permitted when surface is frozen or ambient temperature is less than 40°F**
- When conditions indicate rain may be expected or ambient temperature may be expected to fall below 32°F within 72 hours, no work shall be accomplished
- Notify RPR to be present during application, at least 24 hours prior to application
- Apply soil stabilizer during normal working hours
- **Do not treat soil until all trenching, backfill, for building, structures and underground utilities and pre-wetting has been completed**
- **Do not treat soil that cannot be mixed, placed and compacted in same day**

### Construction Requirements
- Copolymer emulsion treated surface shall be constructed by:
  - Scarifying existing surface to depth of **4 inches**
  - Spreading designated amount of copolymer emulsion on scarified surface
  - Mixing until thoroughly and uniformly processed

### Dust Control Application
- Application of soil stabilizer topical seal coat shall be at rate of **100 square feet per gallon (concentrate) diluted with 6 parts water**
- Diluted emulsion shall be applied in **two (2) coats**
- Each successive coat should be applied in timely manner to ensure surface always stays wet with diluted emulsion
- **Do not allow diluted emulsion to dry between coats**
- Surfaces to receive seal coat shall be completely dry and free from water
- Seal coat shall **not be placed within 24 hours** of completion of soil stabilizer treated surface

- Place and compact in layers to achieve minimum 95% standard proctor per ASTM D698 or D1557
- No debris, organic matter, frozen material, or stones >1/2 layer thickness

### Inspection Requirements

**Timing:**
- Initial post-installation inspection by RPR: no sooner than 30 days after completion and final backfill
- Clean/flush all lines prior to inspection

**Camera Inspection (at RPR discretion):**
- Camera with lighting for clear picture of entire pipe periphery
- Center camera vertically and horizontally
- Pan and tilt to 90° angle with pipe axis, rotating 360°
- Video: clear, focused, free from roll/static/distortion

**Maximum Allowable Deflection:**
- Corrugated Metal Pipe: 5%
- Concrete Lined CMP: 3%
- Thermoplastic Pipe: 5%
- Fiberglass: 5%

**Deflection Remediation:**
- Remove and replace pipe with excessive deflection
- Isolated areas may exceed by 2.5% with RPR concurrence

## 33 44 13 - Manholes, Catch Basins, Inlets and Inspection Holes (FAA Item D-751)

### Field Installation Procedures

**Pre-Construction Submittals:**
- Submit precast concrete manholes, catch basins, utility structures
- Submit cast-in-place concrete structures, reinforcement, steps/ladders
- Submit manhole and catch basin frames, grates, covers
- All shop drawings stamped by CA-licensed professional engineer

### Excavation

**Unclassified Excavation:**
- Excavate to lines/grades/elevations shown on plans or staked by RPR
- Sufficient size for full width/length of structure/footings
- Remove boulders, logs, objectionable material
- Clean rock/hard foundation of loose material, cut to firm surface
- Do not disturb bottom where concrete rests on non-rock; excavate to final grade immediately before concrete/reinforcing placed

**Bracing/Sheathing/Shoring:**
- Provide all bracing/sheathing/shoring necessary for safety and conformance to laws
- Remove after structure completion without disturbing/damaging finished masonry

**Approval:**
- Notify RPR after each excavation completion
- No concrete/reinforcing steel placed until RPR approves depth and foundation material character

### Brick Structures

**Foundations:**
- Place prepared foundation after excavation completed and accepted
- Base: reinforced concrete per Item P-610 (unless otherwise specified)

**Laying Brick:**
- Clean and thoroughly wet brick before laying (no appreciable water absorption at laying)
- Lay in freshly made mortar
- Mortar not used within 45 minutes after water added: discard
- No retempering permitted
- All bed and head joints filled solid with mortar

**Pointing:**
- Face joints neatly struck using weather-struck joint
- All joints finished properly as laying progresses

**Cleaning:**
- Thoroughly clean all exterior surfaces by scrubbing and washing with water
- If necessary: use 5% muriatic acid solution, rinse with liberal water quantities

### Concrete Structures

**Cast-in-Place:**
- Build on prepared foundations conforming to dimensions/shape on plans
- Conform to Item P-610 requirements
- Place reinforcement as shown on plans, approved by RPR before concrete placed

**Invert Channels:**
- Construct and shape accurately: smooth, uniform, minimum flow resistance
- Interior bottom sloped to outlet

### Precast Concrete Structures

**Plant Certification:**
- Furnished by plant meeting National Precast Concrete Association Plant Certification or RPR-approved third-party certification

**Installation:**
- Conform to ASTM C478
- Construct on prepared or previously placed slab foundations
- Conform to dimensions/locations on plans
- Joints between risers and tops: full-bedded in cement mortar, smoothed uniform surface interior/exterior
- Flow lines: smooth, uniform, minimum flow resistance
- Metal or metal-encapsulated steps embedded/built into sidewalls per ASTM C478 at 12" vertical intervals

### Frames, Covers, Grates, and Fittings

**Placement:**
- Place in positions indicated on plans or directed by RPR
- Set true to line and elevation
- If set in concrete/mortar: all anchors/bolts in place before concrete/mortar placed
- Do not disturb until mortar/concrete set

**Curing:**
- Allow concrete/mortar to harden 7 days before grates/covers placed and fastened down

### Steps and Ladders

**Installation:**
- Install as shown on plans or directed by RPR
- If set in concrete: place and secure before concrete placed
- If in brick masonry: place as masonry built
- Do not disturb or use until concrete/mortar hardened at least 7 days
- After 7 days: clean and paint (unless galvanized)

**Precast Concrete Structures:**
- Meet ASTM C478 requirements
- Cast into side sections at manufacturing or set in place after erection by drilling holes and cementing steps in place

### Backfilling

**Procedure:**
- After structure completion: backfill with approved material
- Horizontal layers not exceeding 8" (200mm) loose depth
- Compact to density required in Item P-152
- Deposit each layer evenly around structure to approximately same elevation
- Top of fill meets elevation on plans or as directed

**Timing:**
- Do not backfill against any structure until RPR approval
- Concrete structures: no approval until concrete in place 7 days OR tests establish sufficient strength to withstand backfill pressure and placing methods

**Airfield Areas:**
- Structures within runway safety area, POFA, RPZ, etc.: elevation set to meet grading requirements
- Structures not exceeding 3" (75mm) above surrounding area elevation

### Cleaning and Restoration

- Dispose of all surplus material, dirt, rubbish from site after backfill completion
- Restore all disturbed areas to original condition
- Remove all tools/equipment, leaving site free, clear, in good condition
